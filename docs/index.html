      User guide

      What can you do here, and how?

      Search, read, browse, change the day of working, and compare calculations.

      01

      Open the site and get today

      The site immediately determines the current Pastafari day for the active observer location.

      02

      Search in any available calendar

      Choose a calendar, fill its fields, and show the date.

      03

      Read the date

      Each tile shows the year, cutlet, and month in three lines.

      04

      Browse without selecting by accident

      Use the cutlet navigation buttons.

      05

      Change the day of working

      Open the calculation options to change it.

      06

      Compare the same days twice

      On desktop, align each target day under two calculations.

      07

      Explore the whole year

      Below the cutlet, inspect the year's length, cutlets, and woven months.

      Grid rows are visual only; comparison-table rows deliberately align the same day.
