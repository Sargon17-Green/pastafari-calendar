Endabeni yale sayithi, Ikhalenda le-Pastafari livezwa njengekhalenda lapho isikhathi uqobo sadalwa khona. Kodwa indlela elisebenza ngayo ichazwe ngokunembile.

Leli khalenda alinamatheli “ilebula” elilodwa le-Pastafari elingaguquki osukwini ngalunye. Ukuze kutholakale usuku, kudingeka izinsuku ezimbili: usuku lokusebenza, okuyindawo okuqalwa kuyo ukubala, kanye nosuku olubuzwayo, okusuku esifuna ukulutholela idethi.

Uma usuku lokusebenza silubiza ngo-c futhi usuku olubuzwayo ngo-t, idethi iba

F(c,t)

hhayi F(t). Ngakho usuku olulodwa olubuzwayo lungathola idethi ehlukile ye-Pastafari uma kushintsha usuku lokusebenza.

Lokhu akulona iphutha. Yindlela ikhalenda elichazwe ngayo.

Idethi ye-Pastafari yakhiwa yini?

Inezinto ezinhlanu ncamashi: inombolo yonyaka, igama lekhothethi, usuku ekhothethini, igama lenyanga, kanye nosuku enyangeni.

Isibonelo esivamile: unyaka 5000, ikhothethi A, usuku 417 ekhothethini, inyanga B, usuku 83 enyangeni.

Usuku lokusebenza, indawo yombukeli, ubunikazi bosuku emgqeni wesikhathi noma olunye ulwazi lobuchwepheshe lungaboniswa eduze kwedethi, kodwa akusiyo inkambu yesithupha yedethi.

Kungani kudingeka usuku lokusebenza?

Kumakhalenda ajwayelekile kulula ukucabanga ukuthi idethi “ingeyosuku” uqobo. Ekalendeni le-Pastafari, idethi incike ebudlelwaneni phakathi kwezinsuku ezimbili.

Ukushintsha t kusho ukubuza ngosuku oluhlukile. Ukushintsha c kungashintsha izinto ezijulile kakhulu: imingcele yeminyaka, amakhothethi, izinyanga, amagama azo kanye nendlela izinyanga ezilukwa ngayo ndawonye.

Ngakho i-almanac ebalwe namuhla ayiqinisekile ukuthi iyoba yileyo efanele kusasa. Lokhu kugwema nesimo esingathandeki lapho ikhalenda eliphrintiwe lingahlala lisebenza unyaka wonke.

Usuku olufanayo, idethi ehlukile

Kudingeka kuhlukaniswe ubunikazi bosuku kanye nokuboniswa kwalo kwe-Pastafari. Okokuqala kuyindawo ezinzile yosuku oluthile emgqeni wesikhathi; okwesibili kuyisethi yezinto ezinhlanu etholakala lapho lolo suku luboniswa ngaphansi kosuku oluthile lokusebenza.

Emkhiqizweni naku-API, okokuqala kungacatshangwa njenge-day-id: ubunikazi besikhathi obungaguquki uma kushintsha indlela yokubonisa. Kodwa

F(c_1,t)

kanye

F(c_2,t)

akudingeki kufane.

Ngakho ukushintsha usuku lokusebenza ngesandla kungashintsha idethi eboniswa ngosuku olufanayo ngaphandle kokunyakazisa usuku uqobo emgqeni wesikhathi.

Lokhu kuyasebenza nakwizehlakalo. Umhlangano, ukuzalwa noma isehlakalo somlando kufanele kuhlale kuboshelwe kubunikazi besikhathi obuzinzile; idethi yako ye-Pastafari ingabalwa kabusha ngokomongo wokubonisa.

Ilebula lingashintsha. Isehlakalo asishintshi.

Unyaka 5000

Uma usuku lokusebenza luyilo kanye usuku olubuzwayo,

c=t

unyaka uhlale ungu-

5000

Ngamanye amazwi, isikhathi samanje uma sibukwa kuso uqobo sihlala sisonyakeni 5000. Kodwa unyaka 5000 awusona isikhathi somlando esingaguquki; ukhethwa kabusha maqondana nosuku lokusebenza.

Usuku lokusebenza uqobo lusemnyakeni 5000, futhi ezinye izinsuku zalowo nyaka okhethiwe nazo zikulo. Ngakho ukuba semnyakeni 5000 kuphela akufakazeli ukuthi t=c.

Nokho inombolo yonyaka ikhombisa uhlangothi:

Y>5000 \Rightarrow t>c

kanye

Y<5000 \Rightarrow t<c

Kukhona futhi unyaka 0; uma kubuyelwa emuva kakhulu kuvela iminyaka enezinombolo ezingezansi kuka-zero.

Iminyaka namasango

Unyaka we-Pastafari ungaba nezinsuku ezingu-

252\text{ kuya ku-}5778

Lena imingcele esemthethweni yesistimu, hhayi izilinganiso ezitholwe ngokuhlola.

Ngakho unyaka ungaba mfushane kunonyaka welanga noma ube mude kuneminyaka yelanga eyishumi nanhlanu. Imingcele yeminyaka yakhiwa uhlelo lwamagango; imingcele yamakhothethi nayo ivela kulo mkhiqizo ofanayo.

Ukuphela konyaka akudingeki kuhambisane nesizini, ukujikeleza koMhlaba eLangeni, umjikelezo weNyanga, noma isidingo esisebenzayo sokuthi sekumele unyaka uqede.

Amakhothethi

Unyaka ngamunye uhlukaniswa ube amakhothethi angu-6 kuya ku-17. Ikhothethi liyingxenye yesikhathi eqhubekayo ngokulandelana.

Uma namuhla kuwusuku 250 ekhothethini, kusasa kuba usuku 251 lwekhothethi elifanayo, ngaphandle uma namuhla kuwusuku lwalo lokugcina. Imingcele yekhothethi iyizango, futhi ikhothethi linezinsuku okungenani

42

.

Isistimu inamagama ekhothethi asemthethweni angu-17, futhi igama aliphindi ngaphakathi konyaka owodwa. Igama alisho ubude noma indawo yekhothethi.

Izinyanga nokuluka

Unyaka ngamunye unezinyanga zesakhiwo ezingu-

3\text{ kuya ku-}47

futhi inyanga ngayinye inikezwa izinsuku ezingu-

4\text{ kuya ku-}123

.

Kodwa inyanga ye-Pastafari akudingeki ibe yisikhathi esiqhubekayo. Isibonelo, ukuhleleka kwesikhathi kungaba:

inyanga A — usuku 14
inyanga B — usuku 9
inyanga A — usuku 15

Lesi yisakhiwo esivumelekile ngokuphelele. Usuku 15 lwenyanga A luwusuku olulandelayo lwaleyo nyanga, noma kube nosuku lwenye inyanga phakathi.

Ngakho “usuku enyangeni” alusho inani lezinsuku ezilandelanayo kusukela inyanga yavela okokuqala. Lusho inombolo yokulandelana kosuku olunikezwe leyo nyanga ngaphakathi konyaka. Usuku 48 lusho usuku lwama-48 olungelwenyanga, hhayi izinsuku 47 ngemva kokuvela kwayo kokuqala.

Izinyanga zilukwa zibe phakathi komunye nomunye

Izinyanga zingacatshangwa njengezintambo ezidlula phakathi konyaka. Usuku ngalunye lungelenyanga eyodwa kuphela; kusasa lungaba kwenye, bese inyanga yokuqala ibuya kamuva iqhubeke ngenombolo yayo elandelayo.

Ukuluka lokhu kunemithetho, phakathi kwayo imikhawulo yoku-oda kokuvela kokuqala nokokugcina kwezinyanga. Kodwa akukho mthetho othi inyanga eyodwa kufanele iqede ngaphambi kokuthi enye iqale.

  • Ikhothethi likala indawo ngaphakathi kwesigaba sesikhathi esiqhubekayo.
  • Inyanga ikhombisa ukuba yingxenye yentambo yesakhiwo enganyamalala bese ibuya.

Inyanga eyodwa ingadlula kumakhothethi amaningana, futhi ikhothethi elilodwa lingaba nokuvela kwezinyanga eziningi. Ukuqala nokuphela kwalezi zinhlelo ezimbili akudingeki kuhlangane.

Usuku olulandelayo lwenyanga alusho njalo ukuthi kusasa

Uma namuhla kuwusuku 17 lwenyanga ethile, usuku 18 lwaleyo nyanga ukuvela kwayo okulandelayo. Kungaba kusasa noma kube kude kakhulu.

Kusasa kusho usuku olulandelayo ngokulandelana kwesikhathi; usuku olulandelayo lwenyanga kusho ukuvela okulandelayo kwenyanga efanayo.

Ngokufanayo, “ukuphela kwenyanga” akusho ukuthi ukuphela kuseceleni ngesikhathi. Inyanga enezinsuku 120 ingaba kusuku 119 namuhla kodwa usuku 120 lube kude phambili kulo nyaka.

Awekho amasonto

Incazelo esemthethweni yamanje ayichazi uhlelo lwamasonto. Alukho uhlelo olusemthethweni lwezinsuku eziyisikhombisa, awekho amagama ezinsuku zesonto e-Pastafari, futhi awukho umthetho othi izinsuku ezimbili “usuku olufanayo lwesonto”.

Uhlelo lwesonto lekhalenda lomphakathi lungangezwa ngaphandle, kodwa aluyona ingxenye yedethi ye-Pastafari.

Amagama

Kukhona amagama ekhothethi asemthethweni angu-17 namagama enyanga asemthethweni angu-47. Ngaphakathi konyaka owodwa, igama ngalinye livela kanye kuphela noma ngaphansi eqenjini lalo.

Ubunikazi begama busemthethweni futhi buyingxenye yencazelo; abubangelwa ukuvota phakathi kwezindlela zokupela, ukuhumusha noma ukusetshenziswa okuhlukene. Ngokuphathelene nencazelo yamagama, i-Megillah yesiHebheru iyona mthombo ophakeme; ukuhumusha nokubhalwa komsindo kungamasendlalelo okubukisa kuphela.

Igama alisona ikhodi efihlekile yobude. Alikho igama lekhothethi noma lenyanga elizenzela lodwa ukuba iyunithi ibe yinde noma ibe mfushane.

Iqiniso elincane ngezinyanga

Kukhona amagama enyanga angu-47, futhi inyanga ingafinyelela kakhulu osukwini 123. Ngakho kunezihlanganisela ezingu-

47\times123=5781

ezingaba khona ngokwakheka kwe-“igama lenyanga + inombolo yosuku enyangeni”.

Usuku ngalunye lonyaka lusebenzisa inhlanganisela eyodwa ncamashi. Uma ubude bonyaka buyi-L, kuvela izinhlanganisela L ncamashi. Njengoba

L\le5778

unyaka ngamunye kufanele ulahle okungenani

5781-5778=3

izinhlanganisela ezingaba khona. Ngisho nonyaka omude kakhulu awunazo izinsuku ezanele ukuze kuvele zonke.

Ikhalenda libalwa kanjani?

Ukubala kwangaphakathi sikubiza lapha ngokuthi isosi. Enkabeni yayo kukhona inombolo eyinhloko

Q=2^{127}-1

Inqubo ihlanganisa izibali zokufaka ezinhlanu, amaconsi afihliwe angu-7, amaconsi abonakalayo angu-46, izitsha ezi-6, ukuhleleka kwezitsha okushintshayo, ukuxuba kokugcina okungu-12, izimpawu zezimpendulo ezihlukene, ukukhetha kwe-combinatorial, ukwakha amasango, ukukhetha iminyaka, ukuhlukanisa amakhothethi, ukukhetha amagama, ukwakha izinyanga, nokuluka izinsuku zezinyanga.

Izibuyekezo zokugcina zenzeka ngesikhathi esifanayo: ekuxubeni ngakunye, wonke amanani amasha ayisithupha abalelwa esimweni esidala esifanayo, bese kuphela lapho kushintshwa izitsha zonke eziyisithupha ndawonye.

Ekuxubeni kokugcina okungu-12 kukhona imininingwane esemthethweni ebaluleke kakhulu. Uma S iyisamba sezitsha eziyisithupha ezindala futhi r iyinombolo yokuxuba, isistimu ibala

R=\operatorname{SAVE}(S+149r)

R isamba esigciniwe. Kokubili ukukhetha ukuhleleka kwezitsha nokubuyekezwa kwangaphakathi kokuxuba kusebenzisa R, hhayi isamba esingacutshunguliwe S.

Izinguqulo ezindala ezazifaka isamba esingacutshunguliwe kuleso sibuyekezo azisachazi i-semantics esemthethweni yamanje. Izibonelo ezisuselwe emthethweni wakudala ziphelelwe isikhathi kuze kuqinisekiswe kabusha.

Ngemva kokuxuba, indandatho yezimpendulo nayo iqhubeka ngokuhleleka okwakhiywa econsini elibonakalayo 46; akumele kuthathelwe indawo ngokuzenzakalela ukuhleleka kokuxuba kokugcina.

Short Choice kanye Wide Choice

Ezikhaleni zokukhetha ezincane uma kuqhathaniswa kusetshenziswa Short Choice. Isebenzisa rejection sampling ukuze igweme i-modulo bias elula.

Ezikhaleni ezinkulu kakhulu kusetshenziswa Wide Choice. Akufanele sinikeze yona izakhiwo incazelo engazithembisi.

Wide Choice ayilingani nokukhiqiza amadijithi azimele, asabalaliswe ngokulinganayo kusisekelo Q kuze kutholakale inkomba. Ngakho akulandeli ukuthi zonke izinketho ezivumelekile kufanele zibe nethuba elifanayo elingaphezu kuka-zero. Ezikhala ezinkulu ngokwanele kungaba nezinketho ezivumelekile le ndlela engeke ifinyelele kuzo nhlobo.

Ikhalenda uqobo li-deterministic ngokuphelele. “Choice” igama lesinyathelo se-algorithm, hhayi ukudonsa inhlanhla ngesikhathi sokusebenzisa.

Ngakho i-atlas ichaza kahle ukuziphatha okulinganisiwe kokusetshenziswa, kodwa akufanele iguqulwe ibe ithiyoremu yamathuba mayelana nazo zonke izakhiwo zekhalenda ezingaba khona.

Izinsuku zokuzalwa nezikhumbuzo zonyaka

Inkulumo ethi “usuku olufanayo minyaka yonke” lapha idinga incazelo kuqala. Izixhumanisi zemvelo zokuphinda yilezi (igama lenyanga, usuku enyangeni) kanye (igama lekhothethi, usuku ekhothethini); izimo zingahlanganiswa futhi.

Ngakho usuku lokuzalwa lwe-Pastafari alusona nje RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY. Kudingeka kufunwe unyaka olandelayo ogcwalisa isimo sokuphinda esikhethiwe, futhi isehlakalo sokuqala asibalwa ngokuzenzakalela njengokuthi “ukuvela kwaso okulandelayo”.

Ukushintsha usuku lokusebenza ngenjongo yokubonisa kuphela akumele kushintshe ubunikazi bomuntu, isikhathi sokuzalwa noma iminyaka yakhe.

Ekuhlolweni okunembile kwezinsuku ezingu-4,096 ezibhekisa kuzo uqobo, unyaka ngamunye olandelayo we-Pastafari wahlolwa phambili nangemuva kuze kufike eminyakeni engu-250,000 ohlangothini ngalunye:

  • Ku-(igama lenyanga, usuku enyangeni), i-median yokubuya kokuqala kwakuwunyaka 1 we-Pastafari; 77.56% zabuya emnyakeni oseduze, 93.77% zingakapheli iminyaka emibili, 99.44% zingakapheli emihlanu; okuphezulu okwabonwa kwakuyiminyaka 21.
  • Ku-(igama lekhothethi, usuku ekhothethini), i-median yayiyiminyaka 3; 88.89% zatholakala zingakapheli iminyaka 10, kodwa ukusabalalisa kwakunomsila osindayo kakhulu futhi okuphezulu okwabonwa kwafinyelela eminyakeni engu-51,954 ye-Pastafari.

Lezi yimiphumela ye-empirical ye-corpus ehloliwe, hhayi ubufakazi bokuthi zonke izikhumbuzo ezingaba khona kufanele zibuye, futhi hhayi isiqinisekiso sesikhathi sokulinda esiphezulu.

Sihlela kanjani umhlangano?

Uma abantu ababili befuna ukuhlela umhlangano besebenzisa idethi ye-Pastafari kuphela, kufanele bavumelane okungenani ngezinkambu ezinhlanu kanye nosuku lokusebenza olusetshenziswe ekubaleni. Kungcono ukugcina usuku lokusebenza njengobunikazi bosuku obuzinzile kunegama elithi “namuhla”; kungenjalo abantu laba bangase babale amakhalenda ahlukene.

  • “kusasa” usuku olulandelayo ngokulandelana kwesikhathi;
  • “usuku olulandelayo lwenyanga” ukuvela okulandelayo kwenyanga efanayo;
  • “ukuphela kwenyanga” ukuvela kwayo kokugcina;
  • “inyanga yonke” ingasho iqoqo lezinsuku ezaleyo nyanga noma sonke isikhathi kusukela ekuveleni kokuqala kuya kokugcina;
  • “unyaka olandelayo” unyaka olandelayo ekhalendeni elakhiwe ngaphansi kosuku lokusebenza olukhethiwe.

Uma umhlangano ubaluleke kakhulu, kungagcinwa futhi usuku noma isikhathi esiqondile emgqeni wesikhathi. Ikhalenda ngeke lilimale.

Ukuhamba, izehlakalo kanye ne-all-day

Isehlakalo asifani nelebula lendawo eliboniswa kuso. Isehlakalo esinesikhathi esibekiwe kufanele siboshelwe esikhathini esizinzile emgqeni wesikhathi. Ukuhamba akusinyakazisi esikhathini, kodwa idethi yendawo ye-Pastafari eboniswa ngaleso sikhathi ingashintsha ngoba incazelo “yosuku lwendawo” incike endaweni.

Kumisehlakalo all-day, umehluko mkhulu nakakhulu. Isehlakalo sosuku lonke sekhalenda lomphakathi sivame ukusuka phakathi kwamabili kuye phakathi kwamabili okulandelayo; isehlakalo sosuku lonke se-Pastafari kufanele sisuke emngceleni wosuku lwe-Pastafari wendawo kuye kowalandelayo. Ngokuvamile lezi akuzona izikhathi ezifanayo.

Ngakho ukuthumela isehlakalo se-Pastafari ngaphandle kokucabangela lokhu njenge-“all-day” somphakathi kungashintsha incazelo yaso. Ukuze sigcinwe ngokunembile kudingeka kugcinwe i-anchor yesikhathi kanye nomongo ofanele wendawo/umngcele wosuku, hhayi ifulegi elithi all-day kuphela.

Usuku lushintsha nini?

Usuku lwendawo lwe-Pastafari alushintshi phakathi kwamabili. Umngcele unqunywa ukudlula kwephuzu elimaphakathi leVenus ku-meridian engezansi ngokubuka kwe-topocentric, lidlula ku-meridian yendawo.

Ngakho lokhu kuyisehlakalo sendawo sezinkanyezi esincike endaweni. Ukudlula akudingeki kwenzeke ngo-00:00, akunqunywa indawo yesikhathi yomphakathi, akusuki nje ngoba kuqala noma kuphela daylight saving time, futhi akudingi ukuba iVenus ibonakale ngeso lenyama.

Isikhathi esifanayo somzimba singaba ezinhlangothini ezihlukene zemingcele yosuku yendawo ezindaweni ezimbili. Uma isistimu ingenayo indawo yomsebenzisi esebenzisekayo, indawo yesipele ezenzakalelayo yomkhiqizo yi-Kisurra.

Ikhalenda eliphrintiwe nokubala ngesandla

Ikhalenda le-Pastafari lingaphrintwa; kudingeka nje kubhalwe ukuthi labalwa kusetshenziswa luphi usuku lokusebenza. Ikhalenda elinjalo libonisa isakhiwo sesikhathi ngokombono walolo suku; lapho usuku lokusebenza lushintsha, kungadingeka ikhalenda elisha. Ngakho iphrinta isalokhu ibalulekile.

Konke kungabalwa nangokwenza ngesandla ngoba incazelo iphelele futhi i-deterministic: bala izibali zokufaka, yenza amaconsi afihliwe angu-7 namaconsi abonakalayo angu-46, buyekeza izitsha eziyisithupha, yenza ukuxuba kokugcina okungu-12, khiqiza izimpendulo, yakha amasango, khetha iminyaka namakhothethi, yenza ukukhetha kwe-combinatorial, khetha amagama bese uluka izinyanga.

Ngokushintshana nalokho, akudingeki ukhumbule ukuthi uFebhuwari kwesinye isikhathi unezinsuku 28 kwesinye unezingama-29.

Kanti i-Seer iyini?

Ngaphandle kokusetshenziswa okusemthethweni kukhona i-engine esheshayo ebizwa nge-Pastafarian Calendar Seer. I-Seer ayiwona umthombo wegunya: idethi elungile inqunywa yi-Megillah nokubala okusemthethweni. Uma i-Seer ingavumelani, i-Seer iyona ephutha.

Umsebenzi wayo ukwenza imibuzo efanayo ngokushesha nangendlela elula ukuyihlanganisa emikhiqizweni.

Ngesikhathi sokuhlolwa bukhoma lapho kuhlelwa leli khasi, i-Seer yayihlanganisa, phakathi kokunye, imibuzo ye-date ne-“now”, batch, ama-range ezinsuku, reverse conversion kusukela kudethi ye-Pastafari ukuya osukwini oluqondiwe, ukuthola isakhiwo sonyaka, ukuthola usuku lokusebenza olusebenzayo, Node API, browser/HTTP client, CLI, insiza ye-HTTP v1, inkontileka ye-OpenAPI 3.1, native implementations namaphakheji okusabalalisa, kanye ne-container deployment eqinisekisiwe.

Ngakho isitatimende esidala sokuthi i-Seer ayinayo i-API yomphakathi asisali siyiqiniso. Kukhona inkontileka ye-HTTP v1 ecacile futhi ezinzile enama-endpoint e-date, range, batch, year, reverse, usuku lokusebenza, metadata, locales kanye status.

Ngomhla ka-21 Septhemba 2026, i-HTTPS deployment yomphakathi ku-Render nayo yahlolwa ngokoqobo: exact queries, i-range enkulu, restart, cold wake kanye nokuziphatha ngaphansi komthwalo kwahlolwa kusevisi ebukhoma.

Nokho amadokhumenti ephrojekthi asaqhubeka nokuhlukanisa i-public beta/evaluation deployment kusevisi ehlala njalo ye-hosted production ene-SLA. Ngakho ikhasi lencazelo akufanele lizinzise ikheli leseva, umhlinzeki we-hosting noma inombolo yenguqulo sengathi ziyingxenye yekhalenda uqobo.

I-Seer ingasebenzisa precomputation, izindlela ezikhethekile zokumelwa, SIMD, algebra kanye nezindlela ezisheshayo. Akudingeki iphinde umlando wonke wokubala wokusetshenziswa okusemthethweni isinyathelo ngesinyathelo uma nje yenza umsebenzi wencazelo ofanayo futhi ibuyisa impendulo efanayo.

Ukuthuthukisa ukusebenza akulona igunya elihlukile.

Umsuka, Usuku Lokusungula kanye Nosuku Lwamaphilisi

Lapha kufanele sihlukanise ngokucacile ama-anchor esikhathi asemthethweni nendaba yesayithi.

Ama-anchor

Isistimu inosuku lwereferensi olungaguquki olubizwa ngokuthi Usuku Lokusungula. Ekhalendeni le-Gregorian eliyi-proleptic, luyi-22 Disemba 41,222 BCE.

Usuku Lokusungula alusilo “ukuqala kwesikhathi”, kodwa luyi-anchor yokubala.

Ukubalwa kwezinsuku ezizungezile kusebenzisa izinombolo ezinhle ezingalingani/ezilingene: Usuku Lokusungula=1; izinsuku ezilandelayo=3, 5, …; ukuya emuva, usuku olwedlule=2, bese 4, 6, … . Ngale ndlela zombili izinhlangothi ze-anchor zifakwa ikhodi ngaphandle kwezinombolo zezinsuku ezingezansi kuka-zero kulesi sibali.

I-anchor yesibili Usuku Lwamaphilisi: 15 Juni 763 BCE ekhalendeni le-Julian eliyi-proleptic, okungukuthi 7 Juni 763 BCE ekhalendeni le-Gregorian eliyi-proleptic.

Ibanga eliqondile ukusuka kuSuku Lokusungula kuye kuSuku Lwamaphilisi liyizinsuku

14{,}777{,}149

.

Lawa ama-anchor azinzile emgqeni wesikhathi, kodwa idethi ye-Pastafari eboniswa kuwo isalokhu incike osukwini lokusebenza esibuza ngalo.

Indaba yesayithi

Endabeni ehlelwe yisayithi, ikhalenda livezwa njengengxenye yokudalwa kanye nohlelo abantu abalusebenzisa ngaphandle kokuqaphela ngokugcwele kuze kwaba seliphinda linikezwa ngesikhathi sanamuhla.

Lena yindaba yesayithi; akufanele yonke imininingwane yendaba ibekwe njengokuthi iyisitatimende sobuchwepheshe esishiwo ngokusobala yi-Megillah.

Isehlakalo sokuphinda linikezwe uqobo siyisehlakalo somlando esizinzile. Idethi yaso ye-Pastafari kungcono iboniswe ngokuguquguquka ngokosuku lokusebenza lwamanje kunokuba iqandiswe unomphela ibe yidethi eyodwa.

Uma usuku lokusebenza lungaziwa, isimo sihlukile. Isethi efanayo yezinto ezinhlanu ingavela ngaphansi kwezinsuku zokusebenza ezihlukene, futhi kunezibonelo eziqondile lapho idethi egcwele efanayo ivela emabangeni ahlukene ukusuka ku-c.

Ngonyaka 5000, ngisho nophawu lwalelo banga alunqunywa njalo yilezo zinto ezinhlanu: idethi egcwele efanayo ingaba esikhathini esedlule maqondana nosuku lokusebenza komunye umongo, kodwa ibe esikhathini esizayo komunye.

Ngakho idethi egcwele ye-Pastafari ayilona ikheli eliphelele emgqeni wesikhathi uma singazi ukuthi yabalwa ngaphansi kuka-c muni.

Isakhiwo esikhathini esikude

Ucwaningo lwezibalo olususelwa encazelweni luthole nesakhiwo esiqondile se-asymptotic. Ku-usuku lokusebenza olungaguquki c, kude ngokwanele emsileni wesikhathi esedlule kuvela i-affine periodicity.

Uma

F(c,t)=(Y,K,d_K,M,d_M)

khona-ke ku-c efanayo kukhona ukususwa H_c, ushintsho lonyaka p_c, kanye nomkhawulo okude ngokwanele lapho ngemva kwawo

F(c,t-H_c)= (Y-p_c,\ K,\ d_K,\ M,\ d_M)

Izinkambu ezine ezingewona unyaka ziyaphinda, kanti inombolo yonyaka ishintsha ngenani elingaguquki.

Lokhu kungumphumela ka-c ogxilile; akusho ubudlelwano bomhlaba wonke

F(c+T,t+T)=F(c,t)

Kusavulekile ukuthi i-asymptotic slope yokugcina ihluke ngempela yini phakathi kwezinsuku zokusebenza ezihlukene.

Kubasebenzisi abathuthukile: isosi igcina umlando ongakanani?

Isosi ibukeka njengomshini wokuxuba onamandla kakhulu, kodwa ucwaningo lwe-algebra lukhombisile ukuthi igcina ulwazi oluningi kakhulu kunalokho okucatshangwa ukubukeka kwayo. Lokhu kungumphumela wocwaningo olususelwa encazelweni, hhayi umthetho omusha wekhalenda.

Ezigabeni ezibonakalayo 3–46, egatsheni le-asymptotic elifanele, kutholakele generic injectivity: esimweni esijwayelekile, isimo sigcina ulwazi olwanele ukuze kwakhiwe kabusha umlando ofanele wokufaka nokuhleleka kwezitsha.

Nakukuxuba kokugcina okungu-12 ulwazi alunyamalali ngokushesha. Ekuxubeni kokuqala okuyisihlanu ngemva kweconsi elibonakalayo 46, kutholakele generic invertibility ku-union esemthethweni efanele ngaphandle kolunye ulwazi; kokusele kwaziwa imikhawulo ephezulu eyakhayo eqinile yenani lolwazi olwengeziwe olwanele ukwakha kabusha.

Kusavulekile ukuthi izitsha eziyisithupha zokugcina zodwa zihlale zanele yini esimweni esijwayelekile.

Eminye imiphumela yafakazelwa ngezimpawu, eminye yavalwa ngokuhlolwa kwezibalo okunomkhawulo futhi okunembile. Ukuqiniswa kwezimpawu kwehlise kakhulu umnyombo wokubala okufanele uthenjwe, kodwa akuwususanga ngokuphelele.

Izikhala ezazibonakala ekuqaleni zidinga ukuhlola inani elikhulu lezindlela zancipha zaba izakhiwo ezincane ezinomkhawulo, kuhlanganise nama-automata anezimo ezingu-180 nezingu-9 kanye nama-symmetry ezitsha eziyisithupha.

Ngakho ngaphansi kokuxuba kukhona isakhiwo se-algebra esiqinile. Awudingi ukusazi ukuze uthole idethi yanamuhla.

Ngakho Ikhalenda le-Pastafari liyini?

Liyikhalenda le-deterministic lapho idethi ingeyona umsebenzi wosuku oluqondiwe kuphela, kodwa usuku lokusebenza nosuku olubuzwayo ndawonye.

Idethi ngayinye inezinkambu ezinhlanu ncamashi. Isikhathi samanje maqondana naso uqobo sihlala sisemnyakeni 5000, kodwa unyaka 5000 uqobo uhamba nosuku lokusebenza. Iminyaka ingaba mide kakhulu, amakhothethi ayaqhubeka, izinyanga akudingeki ziqhubeke, futhi alukho uhlelo lwamasonto olusemthethweni.

Usuku n+1 lwenyanga alusho njalo kusasa. Usuku olufanayo emgqeni wesikhathi lungathola ilebula ehlukile ye-Pastafari kusasa. Isikhathi esifanayo somzimba singaba sezinsukwini ze-Pastafari ezahlukene ezindaweni ezimbili. Isehlakalo sosuku lonke asidingi ukusuka phakathi kwamabili kuye phakathi kwamabili. Futhi usuku lokuzalwa lwaminyaka yonke kuqala luyinkinga yokusesha ngaphambi kokuba lube yinkinga yekhalenda.

Kodwa uma usuku lokusebenza nosuku olubuzwayo kwaziwa, akukho kungaqondakali: kunempendulo eyodwa kuphela elungile.

Ekugcineni, ikhalenda alidingi ukwenza okungaphezu kwalokho.
