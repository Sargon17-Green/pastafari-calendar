Endabeni yale sayithi, Ikhalenda le-Pastafari lichazwa njengekhalenda lapho isikhathi uqobo sadalwa khona. Kodwa indlela elisebenza ngayo ichazwe ngokunembile.

Leli khalenda alinamathelisi “ilebula” elilodwa le-Pastafari elingaguquki osukwini ngalunye. Ukuze kutholakale usuku lwe-Pastafari kudingeka izinsuku ezimbili: usuku lokusebenza, okuyilo iphuzu lokuqala lokubala, kanye nosuku olubuzwayo, okuyilo usuku esifuna ukwazi usuku lwalo lwe-Pastafari.

Uma usuku lokusebenza silubiza ngo-c nosuku olubuzwayo ngo-t, usuku lwe-Pastafari lungu

F(c,t)

hhayi F(t). Ngakho usuku olulodwa olubuzwayo lungathola usuku lwe-Pastafari oluhlukile uma usuku lokusebenza luhlukile.

Lokho akulona iphutha. Yindlela ikhalenda elichazwe ngayo.

Usuku lwe-Pastafari lwakhiwa yini?

Lunezingxenye ezinhlanu kuphela: inombolo yonyaka, igama lekhothlethi, usuku ngaphakathi kwekhothlethi, igama lenyanga, nosuku ngaphakathi kwenyanga.

Isibonelo esijwayelekile: unyaka 5000, ikhothlethi A, usuku 417 ngaphakathi kwekhothlethi, inyanga B, usuku 83 ngaphakathi kwenyanga.

Usuku lokusebenza, indawo yombukeli, ubuwena besikhathi bosuku, noma olunye ulwazi lobuchwepheshe kungaboniswa eduze kosuku, kodwa akuyona ingxenye yesithupha yosuku lwe-Pastafari.

Kungani kudingeka usuku lokusebenza?

Emakhalendeni ajwayelekile kulula ukucabanga ukuthi usuku “lungololo suku” uqobo. Ekhlandeni le-Pastafari, usuku luncike ebudlelwaneni phakathi kwezinsuku ezimbili.

Ukushintsha t kusho ukuthi sibuze ngolunye usuku. Ukushintsha c kujule kakhulu: imingcele yeminyaka, amakhothlethi, izinyanga, amagama awo, nendlela izinyanga ezilukwa ngayo zingashintsha.

Ngakho i-almanac ebalwe namuhla ayiphoqelekile ukuba ibe yileyo efanele kusasa. Ngaleyo ndlela siphinde sigweme isimo esingathandeki lapho ikhalenda eliphrintiwe lihlala lisebenziseka unyaka wonke.

Usuku olufanayo, usuku lwekhalenda oluhlukile

Kudingeka sihlukanise ubuwena bosuku kanye nokumelelwa kwalo kwe-Pastafari. Okokuqala kuyindawo ezinzile yosuku oluthile emgqeni wesikhathi; okwesibili kuyizindinganiso ezinhlanu ezitholakala lapho lolo suku luboniswa ngaphansi kosuku oluthile lokusebenza.

Kumkhiqizo naku-API, okokuqala kungacatshangwa njenge-day-id: ubuwena besikhathi obungashintshi lapho indlela yokubonisa ishintsha. Kodwa

F(c_1,t)

kanye

F(c_2,t)

akudingeki kufane.

Ngakho ukushintsha usuku lokusebenza ngesandla kungashintsha usuku lwe-Pastafari oluboniswa kulolo suku ngaphandle kokususa usuku uqobo emgqeni wesikhathi.

Isimiso esifanayo sisebenza nasemicimbini. Umhlangano, ukuzalwa, noma isehlakalo somlando kufanele sihlale siboshwe kubuwena besikhathi obuzinzile; usuku lwaso lwe-Pastafari lungabalwa kabusha kuye ngomongo wokubonisa.

Ilebula lingashintsha. Isehlakalo asishintshi.

Unyaka 5000

Lapho usuku lokusebenza nolubuzwayo kungusuku olufanayo,

c=t

inombolo yonyaka ihlala ingu

5000

Ngamanye amazwi, okwamanje uma kubhekwa ngokwaso kuhlala kungaphakathi konyaka 5000. Kodwa unyaka 5000 awusona isikhathi somlando esimile; ukhethwa kabusha maqondana nosuku lokusebenza.

Usuku lokusebenza uqobo luseminyakeni 5000, futhi kunjalo nangezinye izinsuku zonyaka ofanayo okhethiwe. Ngakho ukuba semnyakeni 5000 kuphela akufakazeli ukuthi t=c.

Nokho inombolo yonyaka iyasho indlela:

Y>5000 \Rightarrow t>c

futhi

Y<5000 \Rightarrow t<c

Kukhona futhi unyaka 0; uma uya kude emuva kunezinyaka ezinezinombolo ezingaphansi kuka-zero.

Izinyanga ziyalukwa zibe phakathi

Izinyanga zingacatshangwa njengezintambo ezidabula unyaka. Usuku ngalunye lungolwenyanga eyodwa kuphela; kusasa lungolwenye inyanga, bese eyokuqala ibuye kamuva iqhubeke ngenombolo yayo elandelayo.

Lokhu kwelukwa kunemithetho, kuhlanganise nemikhawulo ekuhlelekeni kokuvela kokuqala nokokugcina kwezinyanga. Kodwa akukho mthetho othi inyanga eyodwa kufanele iphele ngaphambi kokuba enye iqale.

  • Ikhothlethi lilinganisa indawo ngaphakathi kwesigaba sesikhathi esiqhubekayo.
  • Inyanga imelela ubulungu entanjeni yesakhiwo enganyamalala bese ibuya.

Inyanga eyodwa ingadlula emakhothlethini amaningana, kanti ikhothlethi elilodwa lingaba nokuvela kwezinyanga eziningi. Ukuqala nokuphela kwalezi zinhlelo ezimbili akudingeki kuhambisane.

Usuku olulandelayo lwenyanga akudingeki lube kusasa

Uma namuhla kuwusuku 17 lwenyanga ethile, usuku 18 lwenyanga efanayo ukuvela kwayo okulandelayo. Kungaba kusasa, noma kube kude kakhulu.

Kusasa kusho usuku olulandelayo ngokokulandelana kwesikhathi; usuku olulandelayo lwenyanga kusho ukuvela okulandelayo kwaleyo nyanga.

Ngokufanayo, “ukuphela kwenyanga” akusho ukuthi ukuphela kuseduze ngokwesikhathi. Inyanga enezinsuku 120 ingaba ngosuku 119 namuhla, kanti usuku 120 lube kude kakhulu ngaphakathi konyaka ofanayo.

Awekho amasonto

Incazelo yekanonikhi yamanje ayichazi uhlelo lwamasonto. Alikho iyunithi yekanonikhi yezinsuku eziyisikhombisa, awekho amagama ezinsuku zesonto e-Pastafari, futhi awukho umthetho owenza izinsuku ezimbili zibe “usuku olufanayo lwesonto”.

Yebo, uhlelo lwesonto lwekhalenda lomphakathi lungangezwa ngaphandle; kodwa alusiyona ingxenye yosuku lwe-Pastafari.

Amagama

Kunamagama amakhothlethi angama-17 akhanonikhi namagama ezinyanga angama-47 akhanonikhi. Onyakeni owodwa, igama ngalinye livela okungenani kanye kuphela emndenini walo.

Ubuwena begama buyikanonikhi futhi bunencazelo; abuwona umphumela wokuvota phakathi kokupela, ukuhumusha noma ukuqalisa okuhlukene. Ngokuphathelene nencazelo yamagama, i-Megillah yesiHebheru iyona mthombo ophezulu; ukuhumusha nokubhala ngezimpawu zolunye ulimi kuyizendlalelo zokubonisa kuphela.

Igama futhi alilona ikhodi efihlekile yobude. Alikho igama elithile lekhothlethi noma lenyanga elenza lona lodwa iyunithi ibe yinde noma ibe mfushane.

Iqiniso elincane ngezinyanga

Kunamagama ezinyanga angama-47, futhi inyanga ingafinyelela osukwini 123. Ngakho kukhona

47\times123=5781

izinhlanganisela ezingaba khona ngokwesakhiwo zokuthi “igama lenyanga + inombolo yosuku enyangeni”.

Usuku ngalunye lonyaka lufeza inhlanganisela eyodwa kuphela. Uma ubude bonyaka bungu-L, kuvela izinhlanganisela ezingu-L ncamashi. Njengoba

L\le5778

unyaka ngamunye kufanele uswele okungenani

5781-5778=3

izinhlanganisela ezingaba khona. Ngisho nonyaka omude kunayo yonke awunazo izinsuku ezanele zokuzisebenzisa zonke.

Ikhalenda libalwa kanjani?

Ukubala kwangaphakathi sikubiza lapha ngokuthi isoso. Enkabeni yalo kunenombolo yokuqala

Q=2^{127}-1

Inqubo ihlanganisa izibali ezinhlanu zokufaka, amaconsi afihlekile angu-7, amaconsi abonakalayo angu-46, izitsha ezingu-6, ukuhleleka kwezistsha okuguqukayo, ukunyakazisa kokugcina okungu-12, izimpawu zezimpendulo ezahlukene, ukukhetha kwe-combinatorial, ukwakhiwa kwamasango, ukukhethwa kweminyaka, ukuhlukaniswa kwamakhothlethi, ukukhethwa kwamagama, ukwakhiwa kwezinyanga, nokwelukwa kwezinsuku zenyanga.

Ukubuyekezwa kokugcina kwenzeka ngesikhathi esisodwa: ekunyakaziseni ngakunye, amanani amasha ayisithupha abalwa esimweni esidala esifanayo, bese izitsha eziyisithupha zishintshwa ndawonye.

Ekushukumiseni kokugcina okungu-12 kunemininingwane yekanonikhi ebaluleke kakhulu. Uma S iyisamba sezitsha eziyisithupha ezindala futhi r iyinombolo yokushukuma, uhlelo lubala

R=\operatorname{SAVE}(S+149r)

R isamba esigciniwe. Kokubili ukukhetha ukuhleleka kwezistsha nokubuyekezwa kwangaphakathi kokushukuma kusebenzisa R, hhayi isamba esingaphekiwe S.

Izinguqulo ezindala ezafaka isamba esingaphekiwe kulokho kubuyekezwa azisachazi i-semantics yekanonikhi yamanje. Izibonelo ezisuselwa emthethweni omdala kufanele zithathwe njengeziphelelwe isikhathi kuze kuqinisekiswe kabusha.

Ngemva kokushukuma, iringi yezimpendulo nayo iqhubeka ngokuhleleka okwakhiywa iconsi elibonakalayo 46; lokho kuhleleka akufanele kushintshwe ngokuzenzakalela ukuhleleka kokushukuma kokugcina.

Short Choice ne-Wide Choice

Ezikhungweni zokukhetha ezincane uma kuqhathaniswa kusetshenziswa Short Choice. Isebenzisa rejection sampling ukuze kugwenywe i-modulo bias elula.

Izikhala ezinkulu kakhulu zisebenzisa Wide Choice. Akufanele inikezwe izakhiwo ezingaqinisekisiwe yincazelo.

Wide Choice ayilingani nokukhiqiza amadijithi azimele, asatshalaliswe ngokulinganayo ku-base-Q kuze kutholakale i-index. Ngakho akunakulandelwa ukuthi yonke inketho evumelekile kufanele ibe nethuba elifanayo elingaphezu kuka-zero. Ezikhundleni ezinkulu ngokwanele kungaba nezinketho ezivumelekile le ndlela engasoze yafinyelela kuzo.

Ikhalenda uqobo lihlala lideterministiki ngokuphelele. “Choice” igama lesigaba se-algorithm, akusikho ukubheja okwenziwa ngesikhathi sokusebenzisa.

Izinsuku zokuzalwa nezikhumbuzo

Inkulumo ethi “usuku olufanayo minyaka yonke” lapha idinga ukuchazwa kuqala. Izixhumanisi zemvelo zokuphindaphinda ngu-(igama lenyanga, usuku enyangeni) kanye no-(igama lekhothlethi, usuku ekhothlethini); izimo zingahlanganiswa futhi.

Ngakho usuku lokuzalwa lwe-Pastafari alusilo nje RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY. Kufanele kutholakale unyaka olandelayo ohlangabezana nesimo sokuphindaphinda esikhethiwe, futhi isehlakalo sokuqala asibalwa ngokuzenzakalela njengokuthi “ukuvela kwaso okulandelayo”.

Ukushintsha usuku lokusebenza ngenhloso yokubonisa kuphela akufanele kushintshe ubuwena bomuntu, umzuzu wokuzalwa noma ubudala.

Ekuskenweni okunembile kwezinsuku ezizikhomba zona ezingu-4,096, wonke unyaka we-Pastafari olandelayo wahlolwa uya phambili nasemuva kuze kube yiminyaka engu-250,000 ohlangothini ngalunye:

  • Ku-(igama lenyanga, usuku enyangeni), imidiyeni yokubuya kokuqala kwakuwunyaka 1 we-Pastafari; 77.56% yabuyela onyakeni oseduze, 93.77% phakathi kweminyaka emibili, 99.44% phakathi kweminyaka emihlanu; inani elikhulu elabonwa kwakuyiminyaka 21.
  • Ku-(igama lekhothlethi, usuku ekhothlethini), imidiyeni yayiyiminyaka 3; 88.89% yatholakala phakathi kweminyaka 10, kodwa ukusabalala kune-heavy tail enzima kakhulu, futhi inani elikhulu elabonwa lafika eminyakeni 51,954 ye-Pastafari.

Lena imiphumela yokuhlola ye-corpus eyahlolwa, hhayi ubufakazi bokuthi zonke izikhumbuzo ezingaba khona nakanjani ziyobuya, futhi akusona isiqinisekiso sesikhathi sokulinda esiphezulu.

Senziwa kanjani isivumelwano sesikhathi?

Uma abantu ababili befuna ukwenza isivumelwano besebenzisa usuku lwe-Pastafari kuphela, kufanele bavumelane okungenani ngezingxenye ezinhlanu zosuku kanye nosuku lokusebenza olusetshenziswe ekubaleni. Kungcono ukugcina usuku lokusebenza njengobuwena bosuku obuzinzile kunokulugcina njengegama elithi “namuhla”; kungenjalo laba bantu bangabala amakhalenda ahlukene.

  • “kusasa” usuku olulandelayo ngokokulandelana kwesikhathi;
  • “usuku olulandelayo lwenyanga” ukuvela okulandelayo kwenyanga efanayo;
  • “ukuphela kwenyanga” ukuvela kwayo kokugcina;
  • “inyanga yonke” kungasho iqoqo lezinsuku zaleyo nyanga noma sonke isikhathi esisukela ekuveleni kokuqala kuya kokugcina;
  • “unyaka olandelayo” unyaka olandelayo ekhalendeni elakhiwe ngaphansi kosuku lokusebenza olukhethiwe.

Esivumelwaneni esibaluleke kakhulu, kungagcinwa futhi usuku noma umzuzu wesikhathi ophelele. Ikhalenda ngeke likuthathe kabi.

Ukuhamba, imicimbi kanye ne-all-day

Isehlakalo kanye nelebula lendawo eliboniswa kuso akuyona into efanayo. Isehlakalo esinesikhathi esinqunyiwe kufanele siboshelwe emzuzwini wesikhathi ozinzile. Ukuhamba akusisusi ngesikhathi, kodwa usuku lwe-Pastafari lendawo oluboniswa ngomzuzu ofanayo lungashintsha ngoba incazelo “yosuku lwendawo” incike endaweni.

Emcimbini all-day, umehluko mkhulu nakakhulu. Umcimbi womphakathi wosuku lonke ngokuvamile usuka phakathi kwamabili uye phakathi kwamabili alandelayo; umcimbi we-Pastafari wosuku lonke kufanele usuke emngceleni wosuku lwe-Pastafari lwendawo uye emngceleni olandelayo. Lokhu akuvamile ukuba yimomenti efanayo.

Ngakho ukuthumela umcimbi we-Pastafari ngokungacabangi njengomcimbi womphakathi “wosuku lonke” kungashintsha incazelo yawo. Ukuwugcina ngokunembile kudinga ukugcina isikhonkwane sesikhathi kanye nomongo ofanele wendawo/umngcele wosuku, hhayi ifulegi le-all-day kuphela.

Usuku lushintsha nini?

Usuku lwe-Pastafari lwendawo alushintshi phakathi kwamabili. Umngcele walo unqunywa ukudlula kwe-topocentric lower meridian kwesikhungo seVenus ku-meridian yendawo.

Ngakho lokhu kuyisehlakalo sezinkanyezi sendawo esincike endaweni. Ukudlula akudingeki kwenzeke ngo-00:00, akunqunywa yisifunda sesikhathi somphakathi, akuhambi ngenxa nje yokuthi daylight saving time iqala noma iphela, futhi akudingi ukuthi iVenus ibonakale ngeso lenyama.

Umzuzu ofanayo womzimba ungaba ezinhlangothini ezahlukene zemingcele yosuku lwendawo ezindaweni ezimbili. Uma uhlelo lungenayo indawo yomsebenzisi engasetshenziswa, indawo yokugcina ezenzakalelayo yomkhiqizo yi-Kisurra.

Ikhalenda eliphrintiwe nokubala ngesandla

Ikhalenda le-Pastafari lingaphrintwa; kudingeka kuphela kushiwo ukuthi libalelwe luphi usuku lokusebenza. Ikhalenda elinjalo libonisa isakhiwo sesikhathi ngokombono walolo suku; lapho usuku lokusebenza lushintsha, kungase kudingeke ikhalenda elisha. Ngakho iphrinta isenomsebenzi.

Konke kungabalwa ngesandla futhi ngoba incazelo iphelele futhi ideterministiki: bala izibali zokufaka, sebenzisa amaconsi afihlekile angu-7 namaconsi abonakalayo angu-46, buyekeza izitsha eziyisithupha, yenza ukushukuma kokugcina okungu-12, khiqiza izimpendulo, yakha amasango, khetha iminyaka namakhothlethi, yenza ukukhetha kwe-combinatorial, khetha amagama, bese weluka izinyanga.

Esikhundleni salokho, awudingi ukukhumbula ukuthi uFebhuwari kwesinye isikhathi unezinsuku 28 kwesinye isikhathi 29.

Kanti i-Seer iyini?

Ngaphandle kokusetshenziswa kwekanonikhi kukhona injini esheshayo ebizwa nge-Pastafarian Calendar Seer. I-Seer ayiwona umthombo wegunya: usuku olulungile lunqunywa yi-Megillah nokubala kwekanonikhi. Uma i-Seer ingavumelani nalokho, i-Seer iyona ephambene.

Umsebenzi wayo ukwenza imibuzo efanayo ngokushesha nangendlela elula ukuyihlanganisa emikhiqizweni.

Ekuqinisekisweni bukhoma okwenziwa ngesikhathi kuhlelwa leli khasi, i-Seer yayihlanganisa, phakathi kokunye, imibuzo ye-date ne-“now”, batch, day ranges, reverse conversion ukusuka osukwini lwe-Pastafari ubuyele osukwini oluqondiwe, ukuthola isakhiwo sonyaka, ukuthola active working day, Node API, browser/HTTP client, CLI, isevisi ye-HTTP v1, inkontileka ye-OpenAPI 3.1, native implementations namaphakheji okusatshalaliswa, kanye ne-container deployment eqinisekisiwe.

Ngakho isitatimende esidala sokuthi i-Seer ayinayo i-API yomphakathi asisalungile. Manje kunenkontileka ye-HTTP v1 ecacile futhi ezinzile enama-endpoint e-date, range, batch, year, reverse, usuku lokusebenza, metadata, locales kanye ne-status.

Ngomhla ka-21 Septhemba 2026, i-deployment ye-HTTPS yomphakathi ku-Render yahlolwa ngokoqobo: exact queries, range enkulu, restart, cold wake nokuziphatha ngaphansi komthwalo kwahlolwa kusevisi ephilayo.

Nokho imibhalo yephrojekthi isahlukanisa i-deployment yomphakathi ye-beta/evaluation nesevisi ye-hosted production yaphakade ene-SLA. Ngakho ikhasi lencazelo yomqondo akufanele liqinise ikheli leseva, umhlinzeki we-hosting noma inombolo yenguqulo sengathi kuyizingxenye zekhalenda uqobo.

I-Seer ingasebenzisa precomputation, ukumelwa okukhethekile, SIMD, algebra nezinye izinqamuleli. Akudingeki ilingise wonke umlando wokubala wokusetshenziswa kwekanonikhi isinyathelo ngesinyathelo, inqobo nje uma yenza umsebenzi ofanayo ngokwencazelo futhi ibuyisa impendulo efanayo.

Ukuthuthukisa ukusebenza akulona elinye igunya.

Umsuka, Usuku Lokusungula kanye Nosuku Lwamaphilisi

Lapha kubaluleke kakhulu ukuhlukanisa izikhonkwane zesikhathi zekanonikhi nendaba yesayithi.

Izikhonkwane

Uhlelo lunosuku oluyinkomba olungashintshi olubizwa Usuku Lokusungula. Ekhlandeni likaGregory eliproletikhi, lungu-22 Disemba 41,222 BCE.

Usuku Lokusungula akulona “ukuqala kwesikhathi”, kodwa luyisikhonkwane sokubala.

Ukubalwa kwezinsuku ezizungezile kusebenzisa izinombolo eziphozithivu ezingalingani/eziyingxenye ezimbili: Usuku Lokusungula=1; izinsuku ezilandelayo=3, 5, …; uma ubuyela emuva, usuku olwandulelayo=2, bese 4, 6, … . Ngale ndlela izinhlangothi zombili zesikhonkwane zingafakwa ngaphandle kokusebenzisa izinombolo zosuku ezingaphansi kuka-zero kulesi sibali.

Esinye isikhonkwane Usuku Lwamaphilisi: 15 Juni 763 BCE ekhalendeni likaJulian eliproletikhi, okungu-7 Juni 763 BCE ekhalendeni likaGregory eliproletikhi.

Ibanga eliqondile kusukela Osukwini Lokusungula kuya Osukwini Lwamaphilisi lingu

14{,}777{,}149

izinsuku.

Lezi yizikhonkwane ezizinzile emgqeni wesikhathi, kodwa usuku lwe-Pastafari oluboniswa kuzo lusancike osukwini lokusebenza esibuza sisuka kulo.

Indaba yesayithi

Endabeni yokuhlela yesayithi, ikhalenda lichazwa njengengxenye yokudalwa futhi njengohlelo isintu esasilusebenzisa ngaphandle kokulwazi ngokugcwele kwaze kwaba yilapho ludluliselwa futhi ezikhathini zanamuhla.

Leyo yindaba yesayithi; akufanele yonke imininingwane yayo ixoxwe sengathi iyisitatimende sobuchwepheshe esicaciswe yi-Megillah.

Isehlakalo sokudluliselwa kabusha uqobo siyisehlakalo somlando esizinzile. Usuku lwaso lwe-Pastafari kungcono luboniswe ngokuguqukayo ngaphansi kosuku lokusebenza lwamanje kunokuba luqiniswe kuze kube phakade lube usuku olulodwa kuphela.

Kubasebenzisi abathuthukile: reverse conversion

Uma usuku lokusebenza c lwaziwa, reverse conversion ibekelwe imingcele eqinile kakhulu. Phakathi konyaka we-Pastafari owaziwayo,

(\text{igama lekhothlethi},\text{usuku ekhothlethini})

ikhomba okungenani usuku olulodwa kuphela. Ngokufanayo naku

(\text{igama lenyanga},\text{usuku enyangeni})

Ngakho usuku lwe-Pastafari oluphelele kanye nosuku lokusebenza olwaziwayo kukhomba usuku oluqondiwe ngendlela eyodwa kuphela.

Uma usuku lokusebenza lungaziwa, isimo sihlukile. Izingxenye ezinhlanu ezifanayo zingavela ngaphansi kwezinsuku zokusebenza ezahlukene, futhi kunezibonelo eziqondile lapho usuku oluphelele olufanayo luvela emabangeni ahlukene ukusuka ku-c.

Ngonyaka 5000, ngisho nophawu lwalelo banga aluhlali lunqunywa yizingxenye ezinhlanu: usuku oluphelele olufanayo lungaba ngaphambi kosuku lokusebenza komunye umongo, futhi lube ngemuva kwalo komunye.

Ngakho usuku lwe-Pastafari oluphelele alulona ikheli eliphelele emgqeni wesikhathi uma singazi ukuthi lubalwe ngaphansi kweyiphi c.

Isakhiwo esikhathini esikude

Ucwaningo lwezibalo olususelwa encazelweni luthole nesakhiwo se-asymptotic esiqondile. Uma usuku lokusebenza lubekiwe c, kude ngokwanele emsileni wesikhathi esedlule kuvela affine periodicity.

Uma

F(c,t)=(Y,K,d_K,M,d_M)

khona-ke kuleyo c efanayo kukhona ukususa H_c, ushintsho lonyaka p_c, nomkhawulo okude ngokwanele lapho ngemva kwawo

F(c,t-H_c)= (Y-p_c,\ K,\ d_K,\ M,\ d_M)

izinkambu ezine ezingewona unyaka ziyaphindaphinda kuyilapho inombolo yonyaka ishintsha ngenani elizinzile.

Lokhu kungumphumela we-c ebekiwe; akusho ubudlelwano bomhlaba wonke

F(c+T,t+T)=F(c,t)

Umbuzo wokuthi i-asymptotic slope yokugcina ihluke ngempela yini phakathi kwezinsuku ezahlukene zokusebenza usavulekile.

Kubasebenzisi abathuthukile: isoso sigcina umlando ongakanani?

Isoso sibonakala njengendlela yokuxuba enamandla kakhulu, kodwa ucwaningo lwe-algebra lukhombisa ukuthi sigcina ulwazi oluningi kakhulu kunalokho ukubukeka kwaso okusikisela. Lona umphumela wocwaningo osuselwa encazelweni, hhayi umthetho omusha wekhalenda.

Ezigabeni ezibonakalayo 3–46, egatsheni le-asymptotic elifanele, generic injectivity isunguliwe: esimweni esijwayelekile, isimo sigcina ulwazi olwanele lokwakha kabusha umlando wokufaka nokuhleleka kwezistsha okufanele.

Ulwazi alusuki ngokushesha nasekushukumiseni kokugcina okungu-12. Ezinyakazisweni ezinhlanu zokuqala emva kweconsi elibonakalayo 46, generic invertibility isunguliwe ku-union yekanonikhi efanele ngaphandle kolwazi oluseceleni olwengeziwe; kokusele, kwaziwa imingcele ephezulu eqinile yokwakha yenani lolwazi oluseceleni olwanele ukubuyisela isimo.

Kusavulekile ukuthi izitsha zokugcina eziyisithupha kuphela zihlale zanele yini ngomqondo ojwayelekile.

Eminye imiphumela ifakazelwe ngokwesimboli, eminye ivalwe ngokuhlolwa kwezibalo okunomkhawulo futhi okuqondile. Ukuqiniswa kwesimboli kwehlise kakhulu inhliziyo yokubala okufanele ithembeke, kodwa akukayiqedi ngokuphelele.

Izikhala ezazibonakala ekuqaleni zidinga ukulandela inani elikhulu kakhulu lezindlela zehliswe zaba izakhiwo ezinomkhawulo ezincane kakhulu, kuhlanganise nama-automata anezimo 180 nezingu-9 kanye nama-symmetry ezitsha eziyisithupha.

Ngaphansi kokuxubana kunesakhiwo se-algebra esiqinile. Awudingi ukusazi ukuze uthole usuku lwanamuhla.

Ngakho empeleni liyini Ikhalenda le-Pastafari?

Ikhalenda elideterministiki lapho usuku lungesona isenzo sosuku oluqondiwe kuphela, kodwa usuku lokusebenza nosuku olubuzwayo ndawonye.

Usuku ngalunye lunezingxenye ezinhlanu kuphela. Okwamanje uma kubhekwa ngokwaso kuhlala kungonyaka 5000, kodwa unyaka 5000 uqobo uhamba nosuku lokusebenza. Iminyaka ingaba mide kakhulu, amakhothlethi ayaqhubeka, izinyanga akudingeki ziqhubeke, futhi alukho uhlelo lweviki lwekanonikhi.

Usuku n+1 lwenyanga akudingeki lube kusasa. Usuku olufanayo ngokokulandelana kwesikhathi lungathola ilebula le-Pastafari elihlukile kusasa. Umzuzu ofanayo womzimba ungaba ezinsukwini ezimbili ze-Pastafari zendawo ezahlukene ezindaweni ezimbili. Umcimbi wosuku lonke akudingeki usuke phakathi kwamabili uye phakathi kwamabili. Futhi usuku lokuzalwa lonyaka kuqala luyinkinga yokusesha, bese kuba inkinga yekhalenda.

Kodwa uma usuku lokusebenza nosuku olubuzwayo kwaziwa, akukho kungaqondakali: kunempendulo eyodwa kuphela elungile.

Ekugcineni, ikhalenda alidingi ukwenza okungaphezu kwalokho.
