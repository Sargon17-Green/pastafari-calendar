# Flying Spaghetti Monster article — external-source audit
Date: 2026-09-27
Status: preliminary source audit; not a canonical ruling

## Purpose

This file separates claims in the recovered Hebrew “About the Flying Spaghetti Monster” draft that are supported by identifiable external Pastafarian sources from claims that appear to be project-specific, community-level, or still unverified.

The canonical corpus being developed separately remains authoritative where this material intersects the Pastafarian calendar. This audit does not promote external material into project canon.

## Claims strongly supported by the checked source set

### Creation by the FSM

The original open letter explicitly presents the Flying Spaghetti Monster as creator of the universe / all that is seen and felt.

Editorial consequence: the article does not need to hedge this external-source fact as though the historical Pastafarian source were ambiguous about whether creation is attributed to the FSM.

### Masculine English pronouns

The open letter repeatedly uses English masculine pronouns for the FSM (“He”, “His”).

Editorial consequence: the draft's observation that Henderson's English uses masculine pronouns is supportable. The separate question of grammatical gender in Hebrew is an editorial/language decision.

### Invisible and able to pass through normal matter

The open letter explicitly says the FSM is invisible and can pass through normal matter.

Editorial consequence: this portion of the “how she looks / how the appendage acts” material has direct source support.

### Measurement interference / apparent age

The open letter explicitly uses carbon dating as an example and says the FSM changes measurement results with a Noodly Appendage. It also says the world was made so that it appears older than it is.

Editorial consequence: the saved draft's measurement-interference and apparent-age motif is rooted in the foundational letter, though details added beyond that source still need separate classification.

### Pirates and climate/disasters

The open letter explicitly claims an inverse relationship between pirate numbers and global temperature and links shrinking pirate numbers to global warming and other natural disasters.

The Church's About page additionally describes Pirates as the original Pastafarians and peaceful explorers whose later criminal image is misinformation.

Editorial consequence: the draft's pirate section has strong source roots, but wording that turns the graph into genuine scientific causal evidence should remain clearly part of the deliberate epistemological joke rather than an unnoticed real-world claim.

### Mountain, trees and a midget

The original letter's postscript includes artwork of the FSM creating a mountain, trees and a midget. Bobby Henderson's site later describes this as a Creation scene / first moments of creation.

Editorial consequence: this triad is externally sourced. A much more detailed chronological creation narrative is not established merely by these sources.

### Friday as a religious holiday

The Church home page and publisher material explicitly identify Friday as a religious holiday.

### Beer Volcano / Stripper Factory in the afterlife

The Church About page and publisher description explicitly mention a Beer Volcano and Stripper Factory as features associated with Pastafarian heaven.

### Pirate regalia

The open letter and Church home/about material support pirate regalia as religious/formal dress.

### The Eight “I'd Really Rather You Didn'ts”

The Gospel is consistently described as containing ten original exhortations/tablets, of which two were dropped, leaving eight. The ECHR decision also records this account while citing the Gospel.

Editorial consequence: the saved draft's Mosey / ten → eight framework is source-supported. Exact wording of the eight should be cited or paraphrased carefully rather than reproduced wholesale.

## Claims with partial/community support but not established as primary-source doctrine in this audit

### “Antipasti” as an adversary

Search located community/forum usage of “antipasti” as a Pastafarian opponent/adversary term, but no checked primary Henderson/Church source establishing the detailed antagonist in the saved draft.

Editorial classification for now: `OPEN_OR_UNVERIFIED` or `PROJECT_CANON` if deliberately adopted later.

Do not describe it as an established Henderson doctrine without a better source.

### “Carbohydrate” as a third gender

The checked source set readily supports describing Pastafarianism/FSM as carbohydrate-based, but did not establish a primary-source doctrine of three genders “male, female, carbohydrate”.

Editorial classification for now: external-community claim not yet verified, or project canon if deliberately adopted.

The grammatical observation that Hebrew “מפלצת” is feminine while Henderson's English uses masculine pronouns remains independently valid.

### “Ramen” as a prayer ending

Community and Church-site user material clearly use “RAmen/Ramen”, but this audit did not yet locate a clean primary doctrinal statement defining it as a required or customary prayer ending.

Treat as likely but not yet fully sourced if the prose makes a strong universal claim.

## Claims not established by the checked external source set

The following elements of the saved Hebrew draft should not be attributed to Henderson or general Pastafarian tradition without additional evidence:

- the detailed sequence “light before sun → land → beer volcano → hangover → accidental second land → later sun/moon/stars”;
- a claim that the FSM individually assigns one noodle to each person, animal or object to produce gravity;
- detailed physiology claims that blood vessels or the digestive tract were designed because the FSM likes long branching/tubular structures;
- a general Pastafarian rule that every proper meal must contain carbohydrate;
- the detailed Antipasti theology and hell serving antipasti instead of pasta;
- the “meal continuation test” with toast;
- the specific catalogue of everyday “signs” and the method of accumulating only successful prayer matches;
- the flood as a world-scale cooking accident;
- the detailed “work, forgetting and repairs” model unless adopted by project canon;
- the claim that choosing the more familiar tradition is a sound way to settle contradictory traditions.

Many of these are plainly useful as deliberate project-written deadpan or epistemology jokes. The issue is provenance, not whether they may exist in the article.

## Logic/science content that should remain explicit in QA

Several sections intentionally instantiate reasoning errors rather than merely discussing them. QA should preserve that structure while ensuring the background science is accurate:

- confirmation bias / selection on successful cases;
- unfalsifiability;
- ad hoc rescue hypotheses;
- mistaking correlation for causation;
- circular or popularity-based support;
- wishful thinking where later additions use it;
- theory replacement / approximation limits in the Newton–Einstein example.

For the Newton–Einstein example, use scientifically accurate language: general relativity supersedes and generalizes Newtonian gravity; Newtonian gravity remains an excellent approximation in its domain. Avoid the unqualified claim that Einstein made Newtonian gravity simply false in every context.

Before reconstructing a final Hebrew FSM article:

1. Preserve the externally sourced material above.
2. Mark unsupported claims as project-canon candidates or editorial jokes rather than silently presenting them as Henderson/community tradition.
3. Recover the later penguin/solar-water-heater appendix and any other post-2026-09-16 additions.
4. Reconcile any calendar-history overlap with the canonical corpus.
5. Only after provenance is stable, perform the final prose pass so the public article can remain direct/deadpan without cluttering it with source caveats.

The public prose does not need to expose this taxonomy. The taxonomy is an editorial control layer.
