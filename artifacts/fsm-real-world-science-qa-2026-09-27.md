## Method

This pass checks only **real-world premises** used by the article.

It does not ask whether the Flying Spaghetti Monster actually performs the described actions. Those are project theology / satire and later corpus material.

It also does not “repair” deliberately invalid inference patterns. The test is narrower:
- are the real scientific facts being fed into the joke accurate enough?
- is an apparently scientific sentence accidentally false in a way the joke does not require?

## S01 — Newtonian gravity versus general relativity
**Status: PASS after staging repair**

Current staging wording says, in substance:
- Newtonian gravity is not the fundamental description;
- Einstein's theory supplies the deeper account;
- Newtonian gravity remains an excellent approximation under ordinary conditions.

This is scientifically sound.

A NASA technical reference explicitly describes Newtonian physics/gravity as a first-order approximation / weak-field limit of metric gravity, and notes that higher precision requires going beyond the Newtonian limit.

Reference:
https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19720022194/downloads/19720022194.pdf

The previous crude wording “Newton was wrong” should not be restored.

## S02 — Human blood vasculature is a branching network
**Status: PASS**

The article's real anatomical premise is that human blood vessels form a long, branching network.

That is well supported:
- the Human Reference Atlas vasculature project describes hundreds of named vessels and their branching connections;
- NCBI material describes the circulatory system as a branching/tree-like network of arteries, smaller vessels and capillaries.

References:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10356915/
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279250/

The article's further claim that the Monster chose this layout because noodles are long/branching is theology, not anatomy.

The Occam's-razor move is deliberately invalid reasoning and should remain classified as such.

## S03 — Removing the vasculature would be fatal
**Status: PASS as deadpan tautology; no special scientific claim needed**

The article jokes that if all veins and arteries were removed and laid end-to-end, the person would die.

The actual circulatory system is required to deliver oxygen/nutrients and remove waste throughout the body. Removing the vascular system is therefore incompatible with continued life.

Reference:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279250/

No numerical “total vessel length” claim is made, which is good: such popular numbers are frequently repeated without a clean universal definition.

## S04 — Rock strata/fossils are evidence of relative geologic age
**Status: PASS**

USGS explains that stratigraphy and fossil succession are used to establish relative geologic time: layered rocks occur in ordered sequences and fossils change through those sequences.

Reference:
https://www.usgs.gov/youth-and-education-in-science/introduction

The article's premise that an apparently old world can contain strata and fossils compatible with an old history is scientifically coherent. The theological claim that those were created already in place is separate.

## S05 — Isotope ratios/radiometric dating
**Status: PASS**

USGS explains that long-lived radioactive parent/daughter isotope systems act as geologic clocks and are used to date rocks/minerals.

References:
https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/geotime/radiometric.html
https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/geologic-age-using-radioactive-decay-determine-geologic-age

The article's phrase `יחסי איזוטופים` is therefore a legitimate shorthand for a real class of age evidence.

## S06 — Tree rings / dendrochronology
**Status: PASS**

National Park Service material describes dendrochronology as assigning calendar years using annual tree-ring patterns and matching overlapping sequences.

References:
https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/how-do-archeologists-date-sites-and-artifacts.htm
https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/tree-rings.htm

The article's “trees with rings” premise is scientifically valid.

## S07 — Light from distant celestial objects takes time to reach us
**Status: PASS**

NASA defines a light-year as the distance light travels in one year and explicitly explains that observing distant objects means seeing light emitted in their past.

References:
https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/what-is-a-light-year/
https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/light-year/en/

Therefore the article's phrase about light already being en route from distant celestial bodies is physically coherent as an “apparent prior history” device.

## S08 — Heredity, mutation and biological change continue through reproduction
**Status: PASS with ordinary scientific reading**

The National Human Genome Research Institute defines evolution as change over time through genomic change and explicitly identifies mutations as a source of genomic variation.

Reference:
https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Evolution

This real biological premise is sound.

The theological proposition that the Monster did not need to create each person individually is outside scientific QA.
