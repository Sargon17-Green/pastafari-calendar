# QA Hausa — matsayin wucin-gadi na duk shafin

## Iyaka

Wannan bita ya shafi `ha-NG` a duk shafin, ba `/about/` kaɗai ba: babban UI, neman kwanan wata, ranar aiki, kwatantawa, kallon shekara, binciken baya, kurakurai da jihohi, jagorar mai amfani, footer, metadata, manifest da rubutun ARIA/samun dama.

## Gyare-gyare

- An ƙara maɓallan contract huɗu da suka ɓace.
- An dawo da cikakkiyar ma’ana a `search.intro`, `settings.intro`, `guide.1.body`, `guide.4.body`, `guide.5.body` da `guide.6.body`.
- An daidaita queried day/date zuwa `ranar da aka tambaya` / `kwanan da aka tambaya`.
- An cire ragowar Turanci na fasaha a `/about/`, ciki har da `rejection sampling`, `modulo bias`, `all-day`, sashen Seer, `reverse conversion`, `affine periodicity`, `generic injectivity` da `generic invertibility`.

## Gates da har yanzu suke buɗe

Wannan fayil **ba ya tabbatar** cewa an sake duba duk shafin a session LLM dabam wanda tattaunawarsa gaba ɗaya ta gudana da Hausa. Saboda haka `linguistic QA` har yanzu yana buɗe.

Haka kuma real render QA a desktop da 390 px mobile, accessibility, PWA/offline da language switching suna nan a buɗe.

## Matsayi

Rubutu, UI da semantic contract sun shirya don gate na gaba. Matsayin da ya dace yanzu shi ne **semantic QA**, ba `linguistic QA` ba.
