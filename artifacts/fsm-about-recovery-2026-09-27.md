## Scientific wording guard for the Newton/Einstein example

Before publication, do not use the bare statement “Einstein disproved Newton” as if Newtonian gravity simply became false in all domains.

The scientifically defensible structure is:

- Newtonian gravity is an excellent approximation in its domain of validity;
- general relativity superseded/generalized it and explains phenomena for which the Newtonian theory is insufficient;
- empirical success of a later theory can therefore overturn the claim that an earlier theory is universally exact without making the earlier approximation useless.

The exact final wording remains editorial and should be reviewed in context.
