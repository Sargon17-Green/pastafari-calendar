# FSM article — recovered editorial principles
Date: 2026-09-27
Status: previously approved editorial rules, preserved for later corpus reconciliation

## Scope

These are not claims that the new canonical corpus has already adopted.

They record **how the separate “About the Flying Spaghetti Monster” article was intentionally being written before the corpus-authority transition**, so a future rewrite does not accidentally destroy approved style or misclassify project-invented theology as accidental error.

## P01 — New theology may be invented, but source-check first

Previously approved rule:

- first search Bobby Henderson / established Pastafarian material;
- if the needed point is not already supplied there, the project may invent new theological facts.

This means external-source research is a provenance step, not a ban on project creation.

## P02 — Invented theology should be causally real, not merely symbolic

Previously approved constraint:

- new theological facts should operate as concrete facts in the world of the article;
- avoid rescuing them by saying they are merely allegorical, metaphorical, symbolic or “just a story”.

Example pattern:
- if gravity is said to be caused by Noodly pressure, treat that as the mechanism in the article's world;
- do not immediately retreat to “this is only a metaphor for gravity”.

The new canonical corpus may later change which facts are true, but the **mode of exposition** remains relevant.

## P03 — Reduce explicit anti-allegory disclaimers

The user explicitly approved reducing sentences that announce “this is not symbolic/metaphorical”.

Preferred technique:
- state the fact dryly and concretely;
- let the factual voice do the work.

This matches the later global public-content rule against meta-narrative distancing.

## P04 — Dry explanatory voice with deadpan punches

Approved style:
- explanatory rather than devotional;
- dry, matter-of-fact;
- technically clear;
- occasional deadpan punchline;
- do not explain the joke immediately after making it.

The article can contain intentionally faulty reasoning while still writing its premises precisely.

## P05 — Historical approved project inventions must not be mistaken for failed source research

Before the new corpus became the future authority, the user explicitly approved adding at least these project-level theological facts/material:

- three gender categories: male, female, carbohydrate;
- personal Noodly pressure as the gravity mechanism;
- human blood-vessel structure linked to the Monster's noodle-like structural habits;
- Anti-Past material;
- central importance of carbohydrates;
- sections built around logical fallacies and cognitive biases.

Therefore source audit findings such as “not found in Henderson” do **not** imply these passages were mistakes.

Correct provenance is:

`PREVIOUSLY_APPROVED_PROJECT_THEOLOGY`

The later corpus-alignment pass must decide whether each survives, changes or is superseded.

## P06 — Calendar material was intentionally reduced

The FSM article was not meant to become another technical calendar manual.

Previously approved direction:
- reduce calendar detail in this article;
- keep the calendar explanation in its dedicated technical/public explanation workstream;
- retain only theological/history overlap needed for the FSM article.

## P07 — Logical errors may be intentional content

Approved additions included:
- confirmation/selection bias;
- unfalsifiability;
- correlation/causation misuse;
- preferred-conclusion reasoning;
- wishful thinking / epistemic activism;
- source/tradition reasoning that may itself be intentionally weak.

QA rule:
- distinguish a deliberate fallacy from an accidental factual or logical error;
- do not “repair” the deliberate fallacy into sound reasoning unless the article loses track of what it is demonstrating.
