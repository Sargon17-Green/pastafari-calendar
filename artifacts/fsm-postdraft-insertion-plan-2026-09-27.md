# FSM post-draft insertion-point plan
Date: 2026-09-27
Status: structural staging only; no canon decision and no public integration

## Purpose

The latest recovered history shows that several 2026-09-17 additions were approved after the saved 2026-09-16 draft but never entered the current consolidated candidate.

This file plans **where** those additions could go if the canonical-corpus reconciliation later says they survive.

It does not decide that they survive.

## Guiding historical constraint

The recovered user instruction described the change as a **concrete ontological opening** for Odin/Thor/Loki and required wishful thinking to be presented up front as a tool.

Therefore the later material should not simply be dumped as an appendix at the end.

## Proposed integration map

### I01 — Concrete ontology / limited gods / problem of evil

**Primary insertion point:** immediately after the opening paragraphs and before `זכר, נקבה או פחמימה`.

Why:
- historical instruction explicitly described it as an ontological opening;
- it establishes early that “divine” does not mean omnipotent/omniscient/perfect;
- that framing prepares the reader for later claims that the Monster can forget, improvise and leave repairs incomplete.

Material to integrate if retained:
- Odin real but not omniscient;
- Thor connected with storms/lightning;
- Loki shape-shifts;
- gods can err, quarrel and lose;
- limited-gods framing of the problem of evil / fewer assumptions.

**Avoid:** turning the section into a general Norse-mythology essay.

### I02 — Short early signal that wishful thinking is a tool

**Primary insertion point:** the same early ontological/framing cluster, probably as one short paragraph rather than the full later analysis.

Historical reason:
- recovered user instruction required wishful thinking to be presented **in advance as a tool**.

Possible function:
- foreshadow that interpretation, preference and thought can themselves participate in later action;
- do not yet explain the full causal chain.

**Full explanation should remain later** in the epistemology cluster.

### I03 — “Description of the mechanism does not exclude an agent”

**Primary insertion point:** after `כיצד פועלת הכבידה בפועל`.

Why:
- it directly follows the article's most detailed mechanism discussion;
- the light-switch analogy prevents a category mistake without interrupting the creation narrative;
- it can explicitly preserve the reverse guard: mechanism existence is not evidence for a particular agent.

Alternative:
- after `העולם שנוצר`, if the final corpus uses that section to explain autonomous versus continuously acted-on processes.

### I04 — Full “epistemic activism” / wishful-thinking section

**Primary insertion point:** after `צירופי מקרים` and before `התערבות בעולם`.

Why:
- signs/prayers/coincidences first demonstrate selective evidence;
- epistemic activism then moves from passive bias to deliberate interpretive choice;
- the next section can return from cognition to action/intervention.

Alternative:
- after `מה אפשר לדעת מתוצאות`, if the final edit wants a tighter philosophy-of-evidence cluster.

Material to preserve if retained:
- desire guides choice, classification, interpretation and model-building;
- exact recovered sentence:
  `לכן wishful thinking הוא, במובן הזה, אקטיביזם אפיסטמי גלוי.`
- thought → decision → action → possible future-world change;
- no magical retroactive alteration of fixed facts;
- self-fulfilling effects are contingent.

### I05 — Preferred conclusion as “evidence”

**Preferred treatment:** a short paragraph inside the full epistemic-activism section, not a standalone section.

Why:
- it is one more deliberately bad weighting rule;
- giving it its own heading would overstate its conceptual independence.

QA guard:
- keep it clearly inside the article's bad epistemology;
- never use it in real project provenance/canon decisions.

### I06 — Disorder among traditions strengthens credibility

**Primary insertion point:** within or immediately after `מסורת, זיכרון ודיוק`.

Why:
- that section already deals with contradictory sources;
- the later approved move is a deliberate inversion: inconsistency itself is made to count positively.

QA guard:
- external Pastafarian sources really do acknowledge contradiction;
- “contradiction therefore increases truth/credibility” is the deliberate fallacy.

### I07 — Relationship to existing “כך הדבר עובד” ending

Do not move all later philosophy into `כך הדבר עובד`.

That ending currently summarizes the project's cosmology/engineering metaphor:
- things are created;
- revisited;
- forgotten;
- repaired;
- some mechanisms continue, others require intervention.

If I01–I06 survive corpus alignment, the final ending may need a **small synthesis sentence**, but should not become a second complete epistemology section.

## Redundancy controls

When integrating, keep these distinctions:

| Existing/later material | Core function |
|---|---|
| סימנים | noticing supportive events |
| תפילות שנענו | one-sided success accounting |
| פיראטים | correlation → causation |
| מה אפשר לדעת מתוצאות | ad hoc rescue / unfalsifiability |
| צירופי מקרים | post-selection / ignored misses |
| mechanism vs agent | causal-level distinction |
| epistemic activism | deliberate interpretive choice + action feedback |
| מסורת, זיכרון ודיוק | provenance conflict |
| disorder strengthens credibility | deliberate inversion of source conflict |
| preferred conclusion as evidence | motivated evidence weighting |

Do not merge sections merely because they are all “about bad reasoning”.

This is a reading-order proposal, not a normative requirement.
