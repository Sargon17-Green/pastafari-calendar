## Scientific background — PASS

### Swimming anatomy

Smithsonian Ocean describes penguins as highly specialized swimmers with torpedo-shaped bodies and powerful flippers; their ancestral wings function as swimming propellers.

Source:
https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/seabirds/penguins

Therefore the appendix's central mechanical contrast — excellent swimming adaptation versus poor fit for human hand-tool interfaces — is sound as comic exposition.

### Not all penguins are Antarctic/cold-climate specialists

Smithsonian explicitly notes that most penguin populations do not live in frigid Antarctica and that many species live in warmer regions.

Sources:
https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/seabirds/penguins
https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/seabirds/penguins-popularity-peril-and-poop

The recovered appendix correctly contains the guard:

> לא כל הפינגווינים הם אנטארקטיים

Preserve it.

### Communication

Animal Diversity Web documents vocal/visual/tactile communication and mate/chick recognition in multiple penguin species.

Examples:
- https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Eudyptes_schlegeli/
- https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Spheniscus_mendiculus/
- https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Aptenodytes_forsteri/

Therefore it is correct to say penguins have real communication systems.

The appendix's claim is deliberately narrower: there is no evidence that those systems encode arbitrary technical/managerial propositions such as procurement or specification changes.

### Social behavior

Penguins are strongly social in many species, often breeding colonially and using individually distinguishable calls.

Source:
https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Sphenisciformes/

This supports the “יתרונות אפשריים” section.

### Q2 — Reading/writing wording is stronger than necessary
**Severity:** Low–Medium

Historical text:

> פינגווינים אינם יודעים לקרוא מסמכים טכניים. הם גם אינם כותבים אותם.

There is no sensible evidence that penguins possess human technical literacy, but this is still better phrased as an absence-of-known-capability claim rather than as though a dedicated experiment exhausted every possibility.

**Staging-safe wording:**

> אין ראיה שפינגווינים קוראים מסמכים טכניים או כותבים אותם.

This preserves the joke and is epistemically cleaner.

### Q3 — Legal-capacity paragraph is jurisdictionally broad
**Severity:** Medium

Historical wording:

> פינגווין אינו יכול לחתום על חוזה במובן המשפטי הרגיל...

The practical point is sound, but legal capacity is jurisdiction-dependent and the article does not need a universal comparative-law claim.

**Staging-safe wording:**

> מערכות משפט ומסחר רגילות מטילות את החוזים, האחריות והחתימה על בני אדם או על ישויות משפטיות מוכרות. פינגווין אינו ממלא בעצמו תפקיד כזה.

This preserves the punchline about the inked footprint while avoiding a false claim of exhaustive worldwide legal research.

### Q4 — “Large and heavy for a penguin to carry” is reasonable but unnecessary as a quantified premise
**Severity:** Low

No exact mass comparison is needed.

A robust formulation is:

> דודי־שמש הם מטענים גדולים וכבדים; נשיאתם באמצעות גוף של פינגווין אינה פתרון שימושי.

### Q5 — Factory heat paragraph is appropriately qualified
**Severity:** PASS

The text says a warm factory, **especially** metalwork/welding areas, *may* be problematic for some species.

It does not claim every factory is uniformly hot or every penguin requires freezing temperatures.

No change required.
