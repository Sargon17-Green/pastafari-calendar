## A04 — “Describing the mechanism does not rule out an agent”

**State:** `APPROVED_CONCEPT`.

A later approved addition used a light-switch style analogy.

Core proposition:
- explaining the mechanism by which an event occurs does not by itself prove that no agent caused or initiated that mechanism;
- knowing the electrical mechanism by which a lamp lights does not prove that nobody operated the switch;
- therefore a complete physical/mechanical description and an agency claim are logically different questions.

This is a conceptual distinction. It must **not** be turned into the invalid stronger inference that identifying a mechanism provides evidence for the proposed agent.

Editorial QA requirement:
- preserve the distinction `mechanism ≠ disproof of agency`;
- do not mutate it into `mechanism ⇒ agency`.

## A05 — Wishful thinking as constructive epistemic activity

**State:** `APPROVED_CONCEPT` with one exact recovered sentence.

Exact recovered wording:

> לכן wishful thinking הוא, במובן הזה, אקטיביזם אפיסטמי גלוי.

The later discussion deliberately reframed wishful thinking, not as passive error, but as openly interventionist selection/interpretation.

Recovered conceptual components:

- when several interpretations are available, choosing one helps determine which interpretation the thinker will accept and act upon;
- the analogy was drawn to interpretive/judicial activism;
- thought influences decisions, and decisions/actions can alter later reality;
- therefore wishful thinking can sometimes become indirectly self-fulfilling through behavior.

Another exact recovered fragment from the discussion:

> נניח שיש לי טקסט משפטי, דתי או פילוסופי שכבר קיים לפניי… וברגע שאני בוחר בין כמה פרשנויות אפשריות, אני קובע מהו הפירוש שאותו אני מוכן לקבל כפירוש הנכון.

### Logic QA

This material must preserve several distinctions:

1. Choosing an interpretation can determine **what one accepts/acts on**; it does not retroactively make every interpretation equally textually justified.
2. Beliefs can causally affect future behavior and thereby future states of the world; that does not make beliefs directly alter already-fixed external facts.
3. A self-fulfilling effect is contingent, not guaranteed.
4. “Epistemic activism” is a deliberate project concept/joke, not a standard scientific or philosophical term being reported as established terminology.
